How it works

One click runs the whole loop on a frozen copy of the twin. Live traffic is untouched until you approve.

1AttackAn adaptive red team fires synthetic fraud at the twin and changes tactics when it's caught.
2AnalyzeThe graph attention network scores every order and shows which shared devices and IPs gave it away.
3HardenThe simulator searches 216 policies and prices every good customer it would inconvenience.
4VerifyThe same attacks are replayed under the winner, so the fix is proven before any money moves.
